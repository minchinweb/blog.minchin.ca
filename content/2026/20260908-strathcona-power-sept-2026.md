## Pricing Update

This section is general notes on electricity and natural gas pricing in
Alberta, looking back at August and forward to September.

The floating price for electricity is bouncing off of the lowest price in a
decade two months ago. So it's up again from last month, but still incredibly
affordable at ~5.4 &cent;/kWh. Floating natural gas is low as well, and
slightly lower than last month (again), but tighter (to fixed prices) at ~$1/GJ
below fixed prices. In short, I remain a fan of both and hope you are signed up
on floating rates.

Fixed prices are relatively low, but have falled across the board this month.
If you want to lock in, now would probably be a good time. Natural gas is
getting very cheap for shorter terms, and so if you want to look in the 1 year
rate for the winter, now would be a good time.

## Generation Progress

A secondary line of the business I've been focused on is building my own
generation capacity, to compliment the retail sales.

For my first 10 kW of generation, I've started the physical install. Permits
(with the City) have been a bit of a gong show, with them being cancelled and
then un-cancelled three times, and still needing manual interversion to be
issued; I'm still waiting on one of the four original permits.

The physical install is taking longer than I would have liked, and I'm stuck
with a yard that is completely turned upside down for now. But the problems are
slowly being solved. If everything goes off as planned, I could have the unit
physically placed by the end of next week! I cross my fingers that it will be
generating by the end of next month.

For the second tranche (of ~70 kW of generation), I'm (frustratingly) waiting
on the interconnect agreements for these. Normally the wire company is expected
to either issue the agreement or issue a formal objection within 14 days; it's
now been months without them doing either.


The third tranche of larger sites (hosting 35 to 150 kW of generation per site)
is at a standstill. For the first site for a 35kW generator, the (property)
loan is proving unfindable. I even had a bank issue a Term Sheet and then
rescind it a couple of days later! The connection agreement here hasn't come
through either. We've taken the initial steps to looking for alternative sites.
